# JobBot Privacy Policy

JobBot is a personal tool used only by its author, Itay Zahor, on his own computer.

- **What it accesses:** read-only access to the author's own Gmail (`gmail.readonly`), used to
  recognize emails about his job applications.
- **Where data goes:** email content is stored only in a local database on the author's machine. Text
  from emails is sent to the Google Gemini API to classify it. Nothing is shared with anyone else,
  sold, or used for advertising.
- **No other users:** the app is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone
  other than its author.
- **Deletion:** access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
